# Quiz: easier to find, better to take

The quiz was only reachable from a note's Study panel, and both the player and the results were bare.

| Part | Decision |
|---|---|
| Starting a quiz | A **Quiz** dialog that can be opened from anywhere: pick a note (recent first, with search), how many questions (5, 10, 15) and which types, then Start. It also lists existing quizzes with the best score and Take or Retake |
| Where it is | A Quiz button in the sidebar's Study group, a Quiz quick action on Home, Library and Flashcards. The phone bar stays at five places: Home has the quick action |
| Player | A wider page with a close button, a segmented progress bar and a running "correct" count; the question in a large card; answers as tiles with A to D badges; keys A-D or 1-4 pick an answer and Enter goes on; right and wrong shown with colour and an icon, never colour alone; the explanation in its own card; a larger box for short answers |
| Results | A score ring with the fraction (`3 / 4`) and a line that matches the score; how it compares with last time; every question reviewed (your answer, the right answer, why); Retake the ones missed, Make cards from wrong answers, Retake, Back to note |
| Retake the ones missed | Makes a new quiz of only the wrong questions (no AI used), titled "<title> · missed" |
| Unchanged | How quizzes are written, marked and saved; XP; the Study panel's Quiz tab |
